# Spade: collectible concepts

Draft concepts for a later content update. These nine items are not in the drop pool yet. All are cosmetic; none multiplies XP or speeds up family approval.

The current game has common, uncommon, rare and mythic tiers. **Ultra rare and legendary would be new tiers**. Before shipping these, add their labels, colours and loot tables together, and choose drop rates through playtesting. Keep Chamber rewards separate from random drops.

## Chamber rewards implemented in this update

These are fixed, sequential rewards, never random drops:

1. **500,000 XP — Dogger:** a black-and-white Boston terrier, with upright ears, a white blaze and a short tail. A companion beside the hero, equipped in the Charm slot. A nod to Marlin.
2. **1,000,000 XP — The Spade:** a green shovel with a luminous green spade blade. Equipped in Hand. A nod to the app icon.
3. **2,500,000 XP — Spade Green:** vibrant `#39FF14` for skin, hair and scarf, with self-lit colour in the scene.

**Mythic Marl** is the final named rank, earned at level 100. Numerical levels continue afterward, so the Chamber gives long-term goals beyond that rank.

The proposed future items should use distinct silhouettes and restrained motion. Dogger and the Chamber's green rewards should keep their special identities.
